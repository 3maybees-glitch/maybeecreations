# Five realms: Faith · Freedom · Frontier · Farlands · Future

The site is a launchpad for printable study maps. Each realm has its own page, accent, and Shopify checkout at https://shop.maybeecreations.com. Laminate a map for repeated use. A later direct-download or site-only bundle (such as a complete treasury) can sit beside the current product URLs; map and guidebook fields stay separate so that can be added without a new catalog shape.

## Realms

- **Faith** — Soul Explorer Bible maps and guides. → `/faith`
- **Freedom** — Liberty Explorer civics and history maps. → `/freedom`
- **Frontier** — Frontier Explorer science, literature, music, and liberty maps. → `/frontier`
- **Farlands** — Farlands Explorer country kits (study map, guidebook, audio tour). → `/farlands`
- **Future** — Tomorrow Explorer AI realm maps and adventure guides. → `/future`

The retired `/fans` route redirects to the home page. One retired story URL redirects to Stories.
